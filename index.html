How I work

The process I follow on every project

Even on a small personal project, I try to run through these four stages rather than jumping straight to screens.

01

Discover

Read the brief closely, look at similar products, and note down the real problem before assuming a solution.

02

Define

03

Design

Wireframes first, then a high-fidelity prototype, tested on a few classmates or friends along the way.

04

Deliver

Clean handoff notes, an interactive prototype, and revisions based on feedback.
